HOW IT WORKS

More than print work. Preparation.

Our process makes sure every page supports your argument, every tab keeps the story straight, and every binder is courtroom-ready.

01

Tell us about the job

Send your exhibits and instructions, or call us. Share your case details and deadline, and we'll email you a secure upload link.

02

Get a plan and a timeline

We review your files and confirm the plan, budget and delivery date before any work starts. You'll hear from us shortly, and confirmation follows once files are received.

03

We build and check

We analyze your exhibits, detect pertinent color and apply consistent labeling. Our production team then verifies every set page by page against your instructions.

04

Walk in ready

Printed binders delivered to your door, or digital sets sent securely. When you arrive at trial, your materials are already pre-checked, organized and ready for review.
